Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kinetic Tock ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Pixed, handles user information.

1. Information Collection and Third-Party Services

Kinetic Tock does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, or phone number.

However, the app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and share certain device and usage data—including your IP address, Advertising ID, device identifiers, app interactions, and crash/diagnostic data—to serve, personalize, and measure ads and prevent fraud.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

2. User Consent & Privacy Choices (EEA, UK, Switzerland & US States)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, Switzerland, or applicable US states, we ask for your consent before using cookies or device identifiers for personalized advertising. You can review or withdraw your consent preferences at any time from the privacy settings option within the app or through your Android device's ad settings.

3. Data Security & Retention

Data collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS) and retained according to Google's data retention policies. Because we do not operate our own user database, we do not store personal user records on external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Kinetic Tock is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
